Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Arkoid website (arkan17.github.io) handles information. Individual apps may have their own privacy notes on their detail pages.

Information we collect

This website does not use accounts, forms, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not collect personal information.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and APK files are served from GitHub Releases. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the font is requested.

Our Android apps

Unless stated otherwise on an app’s page, our apps store data locally on your device and do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Any permissions an app requests are used only for its stated features.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub.
